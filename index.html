Execution Playbook · Core Model

iFVG → CISD → Second Entry

We want to see price manipulate off a key level—running liquidity, raiding stops, and inverting an FVG. Once displacement confirms a Change in State of Delivery (CISD), the mechanical second entry taps the fresh gap: clean trend change, defined risk, zero guessing.

Trader FAQ · About

Frequently Asked Questions

What instruments do you trade?
Primary focus is on equity index futures: NQ (E-mini Nasdaq 100) and ES (E-mini S&P 500), alongside high-relative-volume large-cap tech equities.
What timeframes do you execute on?
Daily and 1-Hour charts determine higher-timeframe narrative, liquidity draws, and key levels. Execution entries are taken strictly on the 1-minute or 30-second charts.
What charting and broker software?
TradingView for technical analysis and session level marking; Tradovate / NinjaTrader for direct market execution.
How do I access the Discord community?
Join the server through the link above. Inside you will find community chat, daily market prep, and access details for the private trading floor, alerts, and execution reviews.
